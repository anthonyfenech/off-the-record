Media Ethics in Sports Journalism - Research Notes

Key Principles of Sports Beat Reporting

  • Objectivity and Independence: Maintaining critical distance from teams and athletes covered
  • Source Protection: Safeguarding confidential sources and honoring off-the-record agreements
  • Attribution Standards: Proper sourcing and verification of information before publication
  • Conflict of Interest: Avoiding personal relationships that compromise reporting integrity
  • Social Media Ethics: Professional conduct on platforms while maintaining journalistic standards
  • Access vs. Independence: Balancing team access needs with editorial independence

AP Style Considerations for Sports Writing

  • Game scores: Home team listed second (Tigers 5, Yankees 3)
  • Player statistics formatting in game stories
  • Use of quotes vs. paraphrasing in feature stories
  • Dateline conventions for road game coverage
  • Capitalization rules for team names and positions
