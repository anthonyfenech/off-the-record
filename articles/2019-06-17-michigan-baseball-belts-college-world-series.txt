OMAHA, Neb. &mdash; The Michigan baseball team is not making a fashion statement in

They aren&rsquo;t taking the field in the College World Series with big belt buckles to be noticed. The belt buckles aren&rsquo;t new. They aren&rsquo;t even belt buckles.

U-M has worn them for the past few years in an attempt to streamline the play-calling process. The players receive a number sign from the dugout, then verify it on the play card.

The numbers call for any number of plays &mdash; baserunning, swinging away, bunting or situational strategy. They also include fun statements, like, &ldquo;Be a dude.&rdquo;

The cards are similar to those football quarterbacks wear on their wristbands. U-M changes the numbers before every game and has even added a fan aspect to it: On Sunday, the program had fans vote on Twitter for an addition to the card.

The big play cards attached to their belts is not the only card they carry: There is another play card for defensive positioning that many keep in their back pocket on the field. But the most obvious card &mdash; the one that many people in Omaha are asking about &mdash; is primarily geared toward offense, when players are at the plate or on the basepaths.
