Every August, the vast majority of players are placed on revocable trade waivers. Today,

With Monday&rsquo;s non-waiver trade deadline passed, teams must now first put players they are considering trading on revocable trade waivers, which allows any of the 29 other teams to place a claim on those players.

If a team claims a player &ndash; in this case, Verlander &ndash; the team would have three options: Pull the player back, let the claiming club take the player and his remaining contract or work out a trade with the claiming club within 48 hours.

Verlander also has 10-and-5 rights, meaning he can still veto any trade.

If the player clears waivers, then he can be traded to any team. Aug. 31 is the deadline for players to be eligible for the postseason. It&rsquo;s unlikely Verlander would get claimed on waivers because a team would risk taking on the rest of his contract, which totals roughly $66 million through 2019.

Tigers general manager Al Avila acknowledged the possibility when speaking to the media at Yankee Stadium on Monday but said, &ldquo;Don&rsquo;t hold your breath.&rdquo;
