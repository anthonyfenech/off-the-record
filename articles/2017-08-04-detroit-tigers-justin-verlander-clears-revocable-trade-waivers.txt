BALTIMORE &ndash; The first hurdle in an unlikely Justin Verlander trade this month has been cleared.

Verlander was placed on revocable trade waivers on Wednesday afternoon,

Through a team spokesperson, Tigers general manager Al Avila declined comment.

Since Verlander passed through waivers &ndash; no surprise because of the roughly $66 million remaining on his contract &ndash; the Tigers are free to trade him this month or in the off-season.

Traditionally, big names like Verlander do not get moved in August. Avila acknowledged as much when speaking to the media on Monday at Yankee Stadium, saying, &ldquo;Don&rsquo;t hold your breath.&rdquo;

But with some teams still in need of front-line starting pitching, it cannot be completely ruled out. Still, the Tigers made it clear in the weeks leading up to the non-waiver trade deadline that they would not be giving him away for a less-than-stellar prospect return. And some players whom the team would be interested in almost certainly won&rsquo;t clear waivers. In order to be traded, players on a team&rsquo;s 40-man roster must clear waivers. Prospects do not have that same requirement.
