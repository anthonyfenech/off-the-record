HEADLINE: Tigers' Jordan Zimmermann makes little progress in simulated game
BYLINE: Anthony Fenech
DATE: 2016-09-20

MINNEAPOLIS – Jordan Zimmermann didn’t make much progress Tuesday afternoon.

“About the same as the other day,” Detroit Tigers manager Brad Ausmus said.

Zimmermann threw his second simulated game in a week’s time before Tuesday night’s series opener against the Twins at Target Field, which didn’t seem to inspire confidence that he would rejoin the starting rotation soon.

“He feels fine, he feels healthy, he just feels inconsistent with his mechanics,” Ausmus said. “I think he’s a little frustrated that he can’t get his mechanics working more consistently.”

Ausmus said the Tigers will huddle with Zimmermann in the coming days to determine the next course of action. He said there was no upside in ruling him out for the remainder of the regular season.

In the simulated game, Zimmermann faced Tigers backups Steven Moya, Dixon Machado, Casey McGehee and John Hicks. He allowed hard contact and was scheduled to throw around 80 pitches.

Zimmermann, who signed a five-year, $110 million contract this off-season, has a 4.94 ERA in 17 starts. He has spent more than two months on the disabled list with a right neck strain.
