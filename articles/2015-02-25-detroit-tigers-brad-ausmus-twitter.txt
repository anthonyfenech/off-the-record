HEADLINE: Tigers' Ausmus joins Twitter: Not stuck in Stone Age
BYLINE: Anthony Fenech
DATE: 2015-02-25
URL: https://www.freep.com/story/sports/mlb/tigers/2015/02/25/detroit-tigers-brad-ausmus-twitter/23989041/
---

LAKELAND, Fla. -- It's the 21st Century, Detroit Tigers manager Brad Ausmus said.

"I gotta move on," he said. "I can't get stuck in the Stone Age."

And at 5:11 p.m. Tuesday evening, he did move on, posting the first tweet to his Twitter account, which is

"Day one message to the team… every pitch, every swing and every play matters" was the tweet.

This morning in TigerTown, the second-year skipper talked about jumping into the social media network.

"Probably not overly active," he said. "I'll probably have a Twitter rant sometime or maybe get in a Twitter feud with like, Taylor Swift. Break the Internet eventually, I'm sure."

Ausmus said he will write his own tweets.

"Everything that is on there will be mine," he said. "That's why I'm saying there probably won't be a lot."

And he said he won't read his mentions.

"I promise you I won't read anything," he said.

Ausmus has had a Twitter account for a while, he said, a private one mainly to keep tabs on his children. He decided to create a public account in the off-season.

"It's here to stay," he said about social media. "It's not going anywhere. If I went into the clubhouse and asked how many guys have a Twitter account, I would say nearly 100% would put their hands in the air. It's part of the world we live in."

And now it's part of Ausmus' world.

. Don't expect much but positive quotes and motivational tweets.
