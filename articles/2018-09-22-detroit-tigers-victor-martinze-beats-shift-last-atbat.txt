The hit, No. 2,153 of Martinez&rsquo;s career, was his last. Martinez, who started the game at first base, walked off the field to a standing ovation &ndash; intercepted for a hug by Royals catcher Salvador Perez, who grew up idolizing Martinez in Venezuela &ndash; and saluted the home crowd.

Fighting back tears, his farewell pregame speech was short: &ldquo;From the bottom of my heart, I can only say thank you. Thank you for being behind us all these years, supporting my teammates, supporting myself. Thank you very much.&rdquo;

Martinez, 37, is regarded as one of the best switch-hitters of this century. In 16 seasons with the Indians, Red Sox and Tigers, Martinez hit .295 with 246 home runs and 1,178 RBIs.

Martinez was a five-time American League All-Star and two-time AL Silver Slugger Award winner.
