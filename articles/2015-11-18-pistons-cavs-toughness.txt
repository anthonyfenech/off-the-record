HEADLINE: Van Gundy pleased with Pistons’ increased toughness
BYLINE: Anthony Fenech
DATE: 2015-11-18
URL: https://www.freep.com/story/sports/nba/pistons/2015/11/18/pistons-cavs-toughness/76015658/
---

The toughness is something the Pistons singled out last off-season and something Stan Van Gundy saw on Tuesday night.

“It was a conscious effort to add those type of guys and they’ve added a lot to us,” Van Gundy said after the team’s practice on Wednesday.

And that toughness was on full display

And it starts with Drummond, Van Gundy said.

“I think Andre is playing consistently harder, No. 1,” he said. “I think Reggie (Jackson) for the most part has raised his defensive effort and then I think (Kentavious Caldwell-Pope), (Anthony Tolliver), guys like that have always been hard-playing guys.

“Then we added a lot of toughness, I think, in Stanley (Johnson) in the draft and Aron Baynes and Ilyasova and Morris, I think all of those guys are really tough, hard-playing guys and I think they’ve helped raise our level of intensity and toughness with the guys we have coming back.”

Morris’ job was an impossible one, defending James, but, “I thought he battled him well,” Van Gundy said. “He didn’t give him anything easy.”

Drummond split two free throws at the line to give the Pistons a lead with 2:07 left to play, and hit two more just before to pull them within striking distance.

But Ilyasova perhaps made the biggest impact of them all down the stretch in the Pistons’ fourth-quarter comeback win, grabbing a rebound against Cleveland’s Tristan Thompson with 22 seconds remaining and the Pistons ahead by one.

“Confidence sort of can ebb and flow a little bit with the young team because coming off four straight losses, I don’t think anybody was feeling too good about themselves,” Van Gundy said. “Last night, it picks you back up.”
